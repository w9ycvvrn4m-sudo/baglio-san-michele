Long-term investment

Ethical investment in a heritage destined to last

Baglio San Michele is not a conventional real-estate operation, nor a tourism project aimed at maximising short-term returns. It is a patrimonial and social regeneration initiative designed for patient capital: family offices, family bankers, impact investors and those aligned with intergenerational logic.

Practical reasons

Hybrid model with controlled scarcity

In Phase 1 the historic masseria is restored and articulated into 14 residential units, largely reserved for permanent residents; a minority share is allocated to selective, immersive hospitality. The guest does not purchase a stay in a resort, but temporarily enters a real community with its own rhythms and rules. This structural scarcity allows high rates and a positioning of rarity rather than volume. Operational equilibrium is considered achievable within 24-36 months of opening, provided disciplined management and gradual start-up.

Cultural and landscape asset

The restoration of an Iblean masseria, carried out according to criteria of authenticity and with a natural climate-control system of historical matrix (fisqiya, salsabil, pergolas, thermal mass, principles inspired by the badgir), generates patrimonial and narrative value that is difficult to replicate. Possible public incentives linked to the recovery of rural heritage and agricultural diversification are added, together with complementary revenues from permaculture, the Archivum Artium and training.

Legal structure oriented towards permanence

The project is conceived and promoted by PSPM Srls (VAT 05844360874, Via Giuseppe Simili 63, 95129 Catania). The intended form for the life of the Baglio — a community cooperative possibly flanked by a permanent patrimonial vehicle (Foundation or Trust, oriented towards a Living Heritage) — is conceived to guarantee the lasting destination of the asset. Any layers of tokenised economic rights (RWA) remain subordinate to this logic of stewardship: not speculative instruments, but tools to bind aligned capital to a non-negotiable destination. The internal credit framework is in Economy of Reciprocity.

Designed replicability

Phase 2 envisages the reconstruction of the portion of the East wing (apartments 16–21) and three rural buildings in Iblean stone on the masseria’s land. Phase 3 contemplates a network of villages based on the same protocol (intentional community, limited selective hospitality, natural climate control, cooperative governance, value-based rootedness). Those who enter the pilot phase are not financing a single property alone: they are participating in the construction of a potentially scalable model.

Ethical reasons and value alignment

Beyond economic return, Baglio San Michele interests capital that recognises a value that is not merely financial. The project addresses, in concrete and non-rhetorical form, some of the most evident fragilities of the present time: the difficulty of forming and maintaining families in urban contexts characterised by high rents, insecurity and daily wear; the compression of entire sectors of work caused by artificial intelligence and structural crises; the loss of cultural and religious continuity in many Western societies.

Offering families stable spaces, cultivable land, more sustainable living costs and an ordered environment is not only a residential service. It is a contribution to the concrete possibility of generating, saving and transmitting. The Archivum Artium and permaculture are not accessories: they are instruments of material autonomy and of intergenerational transmission of skills that remain deeply human. The explicit rootedness in the Christian faith and in Sicilian culture provides a non-negotiable value framework.

For a family office or an investor who thinks in terms of civil and cultural continuity, financing a project of this kind means allocating capital to something that remains and that forms people. It is not philanthropy detached from return, but an investment in which economic return is linked to an asset destined to endure and to a model of life that attempts to rebuild conditions of serenity, independence and transmission.
