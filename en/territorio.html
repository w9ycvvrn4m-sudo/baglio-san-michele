INTEGRATION AND COOPERATION

The Baglio and the Territory

The Baglio San Michele project does not intend to cultivate the sense of community only within itself, but aspires to extend it in a living and generative way also externally, fully integrating into the social, economic and cultural fabric of the Ragusano territory.

This openness is not accessory, but constitutive of its identity as a traditional intentional community rooted in Christian faith and Sicilian culture: a bastion of normality that does not close in on itself, but cooperates for the common good and the prosperity of the entire Ragusano area.

Construction sites and local craftsmen

For this reason, strategic partnerships with companies, artisans and associations from every sector linked to the Baglio’s activities are actively cultivated and encouraged. In Phase 1 of the integral restoration of the 16th-century fortified farmhouse and in Phase 2 of the construction of the twenty new rural buildings in Iblean stone, restoration and new construction works will be entrusted exclusively to external professional companies and craftsmen. The skills of construction companies specialized in historic building interventions, accredited restorers and a wide range of artisans and technicians will therefore be essential.

Professionals and companies involved

Of particular importance are the stone cutters expert in the processing of Modica stone and Iblean territory stone, carpenters, blacksmiths, plumbers, electricians, tilers, floor and wall covering installers, window and door fitters, painters specialized in traditional techniques, technicians for thermal and hydraulic systems, installers of domotics and energy rationalization systems, as well as all other professional figures and artisanal companies operating in conservative restoration and rural construction according to Iblean canons.

Such a broad and transversal list of professions makes it clear that the Baglio San Michele project is capable of arousing the interest and participation of a large number of companies and artisans from the Ragusano territory, who recognize in it a concrete opportunity for qualified work, for the enhancement of their skills and for shared economic growth.

Short supply chain and relations with the territory

Equally important is the relationship with neighboring farms and productive companies in the Ragusano area: fruitful exchanges of goods, services and knowledge will be established, strengthening the short supply chain and local self-sufficiency in line with the Sicilian Slow Living model.

Training, culture and artistic heritage

Transmission of trades

Baglio San Michele is committed to promoting training courses for the intergenerational transmission of rural and artisanal trades, contributing to preserving and enhancing the territory’s heritage of skills.

Enhancement of artistic heritage

The Baglio is committed to opening quality cultural activities to the community and to creating a permanent exhibition of works of art that are currently not museumized, thus contributing to the enhancement of the artistic and identity heritage of the territory.

In this perspective, Baglio San Michele wants to be a living and active part of the social body: a place that does not limit itself to offering stable residence to selected families, but that generates authentic relationships, concrete opportunities and shared beauty.

The precious contribution of local institutions, the Chamber of Commerce, CNA and Confartigianato and all trade associations will be fundamental to making this vision of authentic regeneration concrete, shared and lasting, in a spirit of subsidiarity and Christian solidarity.
