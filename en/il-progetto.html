The Project

The concrete path

Baglio San Michele is a 16th-century fortified farmhouse in the province of Ragusa, regenerated as a stable intentional community rooted in Sicilian Slow Living. The project develops in progressive phases and welcomes families and young singles in formation.

PHASE 0

Operational Design

September 2026 – March/April 2027

Before any building site comes the project. Phase 0 gathers the technical expertise that turns a hypothesis into an executable design: surveys and technical reports by surveyors and architects on the existing baglio, agronomic analysis of the land, permaculture design, the study of energy and water rationalisation, and planning permissions.

This work is what produces the real figures of the project: how many units of each type, which crops the land can sustain, which energy solutions are compatible with a philological restoration. Until Phase 0 is funded and completed, the figures that follow remain indicative.

PHASE 1

Restoration of the Historic Farmhouse

The original core of the farmhouse is recovered through a philological restoration that respects the architectural features of the 16th century: stone vaults, tuff walls, and natural local materials.

14 high-quality residential units are created, distributed between the ground floor and first floor, all with access to the common spaces of the Baglio: apartments for families and individual cells for young singles in formation.

Bronze

45–65 m² • Ideal for couples or singles. Direct access from the courtyard, functional and bright spaces.

Silver

70–95 m² • Perfect for families. Possibility of wooden mezzanine and greater flexibility of spaces.

Gold

105–120 m² • Prestigious unit with strong historical features and privileged position inside the Baglio.

Cells for young singles in formation

Within the 14 units of Phase 1, sober individual cells are also provided for young singles in formation. Each cell offers essential privacy and access to the large common spaces of the Baglio (kitchen, refectory, laundry, study rooms, gym and sauna). The dual model allows families and young people in formation to live together in the same intentional community under the responsibility of the Chapter of the Savi.

PHASE 2

The New Residential Units on 23 Hectares

On the lands of the farmhouse, new residential units in traditional Iblean stone will be built, distributed in the landscape with respect for the environment. Some of the units will have a private plot with vegetable garden, garden and orchard.

Bronze5 units

50–60 m² • Essential and functional solution with possibility of private plot and vegetable garden.

Silver10 units

75–90 m² • Comfortable solution, ideal for large families or those who desire more space.

Gold6 units

105–130 m² • Larger and panoramic version, with privileged view over the Iblean territory.

Transition mechanism between Phase 1 and Phase 2

The project is designed to accompany families over the long term. Those who purchase an apartment in the historic Baglio (Phase 1) and, as the family grows, wish to move to a larger farmhouse (Phase 2), can return the residential unit to the Baglio. The recognized value of the apartment will be deducted from the price of the new farmhouse, according to the rules defined by the Chapter of the Savi.

How life works in the project

Privacy + sharing

Each family unit has a private residence. Young singles in formation live in individual cells and share the common spaces. This creates a balance between intimacy and community life.

Clear governance

The Chapter of the Savi manages common resources, approves new entries, and ensures compliance with the Internal Regulations with anti-speculative clauses.

Mixed economy

Contributions from members, internal micro-supply chains (vegetable garden, processing, workshops) and permanent training. No “hit-and-run” residences.

Intergenerational support

Children and the elderly are at the center. The project encourages the transmission of rural, artisanal and value-based knowledge between generations.
