Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

What is Baglio San Michele?↓

Baglio San Michele is a 16th-century fortified farmhouse in the Ragusano area, in the heart of the Iblean Sicily. The project transforms this historic property into the first nucleus of a traditional intentional community, where selected families and young singles in formation live permanently according to the principles of Sicilian Slow Living, Christian faith and self-sufficiency.

Who is the project aimed at?↓

The project accommodates two complementary modes: families (parents ideally aged 35–50, with one or more children) living in autonomous apartments, and young singles in formation residing in individual cells and sharing large common spaces. Both paths converge in the same traditional intentional community under the responsibility of the Chapter of the Savi. For more details see Who We’re Looking For.

What is the difference between Baglio San Michele and a resort or tourist village?↓

Baglio San Michele is not a resort, not a diffused hotel, and not a seasonal tourist project. It is a place of stable year-round residence, inhabited by families and young people in formation who choose it as their home and adhere to a shared internal regulation.

How does the admission process work?↓

Admission takes place in progressive stages: Application, Introductory interview, Residential visit, Trial period, Formal entry. For full details see How to Join.

How much does it cost to join and how does ownership work?↓

Costs depend on the housing unit (apartment in the historic Baglio, cell or rural house) and the role assumed in the community. The model is based on transparency, anti-speculative clauses and protection of the common good. Economic details are shared only after the application and the introductory interview.

Is it possible to work remotely?↓

Yes. Study spaces and rural coworking are planned, together with essential and regulated connectivity. Remote work is compatible with life at the Baglio, provided it does not compromise participation in common activities and relational life.

How is children’s education managed?↓

Children’s education is a pillar of the project. In addition to the local public school, the Baglio promotes activities for the transmission of crafts, culture and values through the Archivum Artium and intergenerational community life. The goal is to offer a stable, safe environment rich in authentic relationships.

Can singles or young people in formation also apply?↓

Yes. The dual model explicitly provides for young singles in formation, who live in sober individual cells and share large common spaces (kitchen, refectory, laundry, sauna, gym, study rooms). Currently the singles section is reserved for young men; a parallel female section may be evaluated in the future upon motivated requests. Selection remains rigorous and mutual. For more details see Who We’re Looking For.

What happens if the family grows and wants a larger home?↓

A transition mechanism is provided: those living in a Phase 1 apartment may, in case of family growth, return the unit and use its value toward the purchase of one of the Phase 2 rural buildings, according to the rules defined by the Chapter of the Savi.

What are the development phases of the project?↓

Phase 0 (September 2026 – March/April 2027): Operational design — surveys, technical reports and consultancy (surveyors, architects, agronomists, permaculture, energy rationalisation) to move from hypothesis to a working project. Phase 1 (2027-2029): Restoration of the historic Baglio with 14 units (apartments for families + cells for young singles in formation). Phase 2 (post-2029): Reconstruction of the portion of the East Wing (apartments 16–21) and three rural buildings in Iblean stone (135, 85 and 175 m²). Details on the pages The place and The Project.

How is governance organized?↓

Governance provides for a Chapter of the Savi composed of 9 members with specific skills. Learn more in Governance.

How are conflicts within the community handled?↓

Governance provides clear rules, defined decision-making bodies and a mediation path. The Chapter of the Savi has among its tasks the protection of cohesion and the resolution of conflicts according to the principles of reciprocity and shared responsibility.

Is it possible to leave the project?↓

Yes. Entry is not irrevocable. There are regulated exit procedures that protect both the person leaving and the community, avoiding speculative phenomena. Details are set out in the Internal Regulations.

What are the fundamental rules of common life?↓

The main rules concern respect for others’ privacy, participation in common activities, responsibility in the management of spaces and resources, and adherence to the founding values of the project. The complete document is shared upon entry as the Internal Regulations.

What is the role of faith?↓

The Christian spiritual dimension is a founding value of community life. The community is intentionally rooted in traditional Christian faith and Sicilian culture.

How is food and energy autonomy ensured?↓

Food and livestock self-sufficiency is a pillar of the model. See Food Autonomy and Energy Autonomy.

Which professions are required?↓

Professions in agriculture, livestock, artisanal food processing, craftsmanship and restoration. See Professional Roles and Archivum Artium.

How does the economy of reciprocity work?↓

Those who contribute hours of work or services accumulate credits that can be spent on farm products or services from other members. Learn more in Economy of Reciprocity.

What is daily life like at the Baglio?↓

Life follows a slower and more conscious rhythm. Each family has a private home; young singles in formation live in cells and share the common spaces. See Living at the Baglio and Community Life.

How does the Baglio integrate with the surrounding territory?↓

The Baglio collaborates with artisans, farms and businesses of the Ragusano area. See Cooperation.

Is it possible to visit the Baglio?↓

The project is currently in the preparatory phase. Visits will be organized in a structured way once Phase 1 begins. Those interested can apply through the contact form.

Can I support the project without living here?↓

Yes. It is possible to support the Baglio through a donation or by purchasing project tokens. Go to Donations.

Are donations tax-deductible?↓

Not at this stage. Donations are gifts (liberalità), accounted for by PSPM Srls. Once the consortium and the cooperative have been established, we will also open channels for tax-deductible donations. Details in Donations.

Can I bring my pets?↓

Yes. Dogs and cats are welcome provided they do not disturb the neighbors. Learn more in Pets.

Is it possible to keep courtyard animals?↓

It is possible with authorization from the Chapter of the Savi. Courtyard animals are primarily allowed in units with a private lot in Phase 2. See Pets.
