Support the Baglio

Every contribution helps build a place founded on autonomy, ethics, and open community.

Baglio San Michele is more than just a place to live: it is a regeneration project that needs the support of those who believe in a more stable, ethical, and community-oriented future. Your donation concretely contributes to preserving heritage, developing autonomy, and making this vision accessible to more people.

Why donate to Baglio San Michele?

Baglio San Michele is a concrete project of territorial and community regeneration. Donations allow us to accelerate the development of sustainable infrastructure, support food and energy autonomy, and make the opportunity to live and collaborate here accessible to more people.

Every euro donated is used transparently for:

  • Financing executive design and restoration works on the Baglio
  • Contributing to the implementation of the Borgo Ideale tokenization system
  • Preserving and enhancing the historical and landscape heritage of the place
  • Restoring furniture and fittings in the common areas of the farmhouse
