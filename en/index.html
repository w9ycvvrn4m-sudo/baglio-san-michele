The residential units

The project includes two types of housing, designed for different needs but united by the same vision of community and quality of life.

PHASE 1 — IN PROGRESS

Apartments in the Historic Baglio

14 units created through the authentic restoration of the 16th-century fortified farmhouse. Each apartment retains its original architectural features and benefits from the Baglio’s common areas.

Bronze 6 units

45–65 m² • Ideal for couples or singles. Direct access from the courtyard, functional and bright spaces.

Silver 7 units

80–105 m² • Perfect for families. Option for a wooden loft and greater flexibility in the layout.

Gold 1 unit

105–115 m² • Prestigious units with distinctive historical features and a prime location within the Baglio.

PHASE 2 — IN PLANNING

Farmhouses on 23 hectares

20 new homes built in traditional Iblean stone, spread across the farm’s grounds. Each farmhouse will feature a private lot of approximately one hectare, complete with a vegetable garden, yard, and orchard.

Bronze Farmhouses 8 units

140–160 m² • Simple and functional design with a private lot and the possibility of a vegetable garden.

Silver Farmhouses 8 units

200–220 m² • Spacious and comfortable, ideal for large families or those who want more space.

Gold Farmhouses 4 units

240–260 m² • A more spacious version with panoramic views, offering a privileged vista of the Iblean region.

A life project, not just a place to live

Baglio San Michele was created to offer a tangible alternative in times of great uncertainty: a place where families can put down deep roots, raise their children in safety and beauty, cultivate authentic relationships, and contribute to a vibrant community rooted in faith and Sicilian tradition.
