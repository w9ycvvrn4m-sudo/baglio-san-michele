Baglio San Michele

An intentional community rooted in faith, tradition and Sicilian culture

Discover how to live here The place

Why this place is different

It is not just a place to live. It is a life choice, of values and of future for one’s family.

Real stability

A model designed to last for generations, not to generate quick capital gains.

Selected community

Families and young singles in formation who share values, faith and a desire for authentic rootedness.

Tradition and future

A balance between respect for Sicilian tradition and a vision for the future of new generations.

THE PLACE

The farmhouse, the courtyard, the wings

Before the discourse on the project, the stone enclosure: courtyard, South and North wings, East wing to be rebuilt, chapel, entrance hall and three rural buildings on the estate. The drawing is the document from which the space is read.

Read the plan →

The residential units

The project includes two development phases with different housing types: apartments for families and cells for young singles in formation.

PHASE 1

Restoration of the historic Baglio

14 residential units inside the 16th-century farmhouse: apartments for families and cells for young singles in formation.

Bronze: 45–65 m²
Silver: 70–95 m²
Gold: 105–120 m²
Cells: sober individual spaces for young singles in formation, with shared common areas
PHASE 2

East Wing and rural buildings

Reconstruction of the collapsed wing and three bodies in Iblean stone on the estate, outside the historic enclosure.

Building 1: 135 m² · by the south portal
Building 2: 85 m² · south, on the garden
Building 3: 175 m² · west-south
East Wing: apartments 16–21 to be rebuilt from scratch
