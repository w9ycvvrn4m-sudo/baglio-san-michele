The Concept

Our vision

Who we are

We are families and professionals who have chosen to put down roots in Sicily, in the province of Ragusa, to regenerate an ancient fortified farmhouse as a stable intentional community, inspired by Sicilian Slow Living.

The founding values

Stability and continuity

We believe in a long-term residential model, where families can grow and age together without being forced to move continuously.

Authentic relationships

The community is small by choice: we want people to truly know each other, help one another, and create lasting bonds.

Living tradition

We value Sicilian cultural, artisanal, and spiritual heritage as a living resource to be transmitted, not as a museum artifact.

Shared responsibility

Every member contributes according to their abilities and skills, in a balance between individual freedom and the common good.

Respect for the land

Food self-sufficiency, soil care, and responsible use of resources are at the center of our way of life.

Intergenerational transmission

Children and the elderly are active members of the community: the former learn, the latter pass on knowledge and wisdom.

The intentional community model

An intentional community is not simply a condominium or a tourist village. It is a group of people who consciously choose to live together according to shared values, clear rules, and a common life project.

Our areas of focus

Architecture and construction

Philological restoration of the historic farmhouse, use of local materials and bioclimatic criteria. Every intervention is designed to last over time and respect the identity of the place.

Land and livestock

Vegetable garden, orchard, beekeeping and sustainable soil management. The goal is to achieve progressive food self-sufficiency and respect the natural cycle of the land.

Economy and governance

Clear agreements among members, common funds, periodic reporting and anti-speculative rules. Economic transparency is the foundation of mutual trust.

Crafts and supply chains

Wood and stone work, textile supply chains and food processing. We value traditional trades and create job opportunities within the community.

Community and education

Participatory decision-making processes, intergenerational care, activities for children and the elderly. The community is a place of continuous learning and transmission of values.

Remote work and connectivity

Rural coworking spaces and quality connectivity. The project allows balancing professional life and quality of life in a natural and community context.
