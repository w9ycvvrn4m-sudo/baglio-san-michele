VISION AND VALUES

The Concept

Who we are

We are the founders of Baglio San Michele: families and professionals who have chosen to put down roots in Sicily (province of Ragusa) and regenerate a historic farmhouse as a stable intentional community, inspired by Sicilian Slow Living.

Vision

Home, work, relationships and landscape as a single ecosystem.

Method

Small steps, high standards, shared responsibility, economic transparency.

Identity

Explicit and serene spiritual dimension; tradition as a living flame, not ashes to preserve.

Our areas of focus

Architecture and Construction

Philological restoration, local materials, bioclimatic criteria. Quality of living at the service of community life.

Land and Agriculture

Vegetable garden, orchard, beekeeping, animal welfare, water and soil management. Progressive food autonomy.

Economy and Governance

Clear agreements, common funds, periodic reporting. Transparent and anti-speculative economic model.

Crafts and Supply Chains

Wood, stone, textiles, food processing. Enhancement of traditional skills and short supply chains.

Community and Education

Decision-making processes, intergenerational care, activities for children and the elderly. Transmission of rural and artisanal knowledge.

Remote Work

Essential connectivity, study spaces and rural coworking. A balance between professional life and community presence.

We are small by choice

Admission takes place in stages: application, interview, visit, trial period, formal entry. The selection is mutual. We are looking for people who share our vision and are willing to actively contribute to the life of the community.
