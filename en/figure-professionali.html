Work Opportunities

Professional Roles Required

These roles are requested not as employees, but as stable residents who actively contribute to community life, the regeneration of the heritage, and self-sufficiency, with willingness to pass on skills intergenerationally. The selected people will operate as entrepreneurs within the Baglio San Michele cooperative. This model guarantees them economic independence, through the direct management of their own activities (agricultural production, artisanal processing, services or trades), combined with participation in the benefits and decisions of the community. The economic system is strengthened by the tokenization of the Village, which allows transparent distribution of the value generated and aligns long-term interests among all members.

1. Primary agricultural and livestock production

Absolute priority – food self-sufficiency.

  • Biodynamic farmer / peasant (4-6 full-time units). Integrated management of traditional crops with biodynamic and regenerative soil methods.
  • Expert in traditional pruning and arboriculture (at least 2 units). Care of centuries-old olive trees and fruit trees according to historical techniques.
  • Vegetable gardener and seasonal vegetable producer (2-3 units). Management of family and collective vegetable gardens.
  • Specialized poultry farmer (1-2 units).
  • Goat farmer (at least 1 unit).
  • Pig farmer (at least 1 unit).
  • Shepherd / small ruminant farmer (1-2 units).
  • Minor backyard animal farmer (1 unit, integrable).

2. Artisanal food processing

  • Baker (1-2 units with traditional wood-fired oven). Daily production of bread, focaccia and bakery products with local flours and sourdough.
  • Butcher / pork butcher (1 unit). Slaughtering, cured meats production and meat preservation according to traditional Sicilian methods.
  • Cheesemaker (1 unit). Production of cheeses and dairy products from cattle and sheep according to traditional Sicilian methods.
  • Jam and preserve maker (1 unit). Preparation of jams, preserves and sauces according to traditional Sicilian methods.

3. Crafts, restoration and building maintenance

  • Mason / restorer specialized in traditional techniques and Iblean stone work (stone cutters, installers)
  • Carpenter / cabinetmaker / restorer of wooden furniture and artefacts (1 unit).
  • Blacksmith and metalworking artisan (1 unit).
  • Gypsum and stucco artisan (1-2 units).
  • Artisan typographer and bookbinder (1 unit).
  • Mosaicist (1 unit).

Other roles required for the proper functioning of the community

In addition to the agricultural, livestock and artisanal activities already planned, a self-sufficient farmhouse and an intentional community require essential functions for daily operation, maintenance, social cohesion, governance and long-term sustainability.

1. Administration, governance and organizational support

  • Administrative coordinator / accountant
  • Secretary of the Chapter of the Savi
  • Safety and risk prevention officer

2. Maintenance and infrastructure

  • General maintenance / handyman
  • Gardener / landscape custodian
  • Energy systems manager

3. Care of people and social services

  • Nurse / socio-health operator
  • Educator / homeschooling teacher
  • Collective kitchen manager

4. Logistics and operational support

  • Warehouse keeper / supply manager
  • Common laundry attendant

5. Cultural, spiritual and educational activities

  • Sacristan / Ceremonial officer
  • Librarian / archivist
  • Cultural animator / event organizer

6. Communication and external relations

  • Communication and website manager
  • Institutional relations officer

These figures ensure that the farmhouse is not only productive, but also livable, orderly, safe and spiritually cared for. Many can be combined or performed by residents with multiple skills.

School of Arts and Crafts – Archivum Artium

Archivum Artium is the Baglio’s internal school dedicated to the practical transmission of traditional crafts. It offers training courses and internships for residents and young people from the territory, with the aim of preserving and renewing Sicilian artisanal and rural skills.

Tokenized credit system

The Baglio adopts an internal credit system (soon to be tokenized) to value each person’s contribution. Those who work for the community accumulate credits that can be spent on products, services or towards the purchase of housing units. This mechanism strengthens reciprocity and the alignment of interests among all members.
