ADMISSION PROCESS

How to Join the Baglio

A path of mutual knowledge

Joining Baglio San Michele is not simply a real estate purchase. It is a life choice that requires time, mutual understanding, and a deep alignment of values. For this reason, we have structured a clear and gradual admission process, designed to allow both parties to evaluate with seriousness and respect the possibility of walking together.

The process is reciprocal: not only does the Baglio evaluate candidates, but those who wish to join also have the opportunity to get to know the place, the people, and the lifestyle before making a final decision.

The stages of the admission process

1

Application

The process begins with the submission of an application through the form available on the website. Candidates are asked to fill it out carefully, attaching a motivation letter and, if possible, a short curriculum or description of their skills and family composition.

2

Introductory Interview

Selected candidates are invited to an interview (in person or online) with one or more members of the founding group. This meeting serves to get to know each other, explore motivations, clarify doubts, and verify alignment with the principles of the project.

3

Residential Visit

After the interview, candidates are invited to spend a few days at the Baglio. This moment is fundamental to directly experience the atmosphere of the place, meet other residents (when present), and get a concrete idea of community life and the territory.

4

Trial Period

In case of mutual interest, a trial period of variable duration (generally a few months) follows. During this phase, the family lives stably at the Baglio, participating in community life and contributing, according to their possibilities, to the activities of the place. It is a time of deep understanding and concrete verification of integration.

5

Formal Admission

At the end of the trial period, if both parties confirm their willingness to continue together, formal admission takes place. An agreement is signed that regulates the assignment of the residential unit, as well as the rights and duties of the family within the community.

The principles of our process

Reciprocity

The Baglio evaluates candidates just as candidates evaluate the Baglio. Both parties must feel free and motivated to walk together.

Transparency

We strive to be as clear as possible from the beginning about the principles, rules, and expectations of the project.

Graduality

The process is structured in progressive stages precisely to allow everyone to make a conscious and serene decision.

Respect for Time

There are no forced deadlines. We believe that such an important choice deserves the time necessary to be carefully considered.

Are you interested in starting this journey?

If you feel that Baglio San Michele could be the right place for you and your family, we invite you to begin the process by submitting your application.
