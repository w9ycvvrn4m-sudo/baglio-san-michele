An-Nudama

The circle is closed. Membership is by invitation and by the agreement of the house.

What follows is for those who already hold the address.

The threshold

The Munya and the Riad share a wall. One door only, opened from the Riad side. The key is held by the Bawwāb of the house next door.

A man is received if the house accepts him — not because of what he can pay.

The spaces

Sqifa, patio and hall, garden, oratory, guest apartments, apartment of honour, salons, parlour, dining room with its own kitchen, service rooms, store.

The house is furnished with care. It is not a hotel.

Admission

Admission is decided by the house. A stay may be granted provided that each time a man sits before the house and the house says yes.

The register

The names of members exist in no digital form.

The roll, the acts of admission, the signatures, the designations of successors and the removals are kept by hand in a single register, held in the treasury of the house next door. No website, no list, no database, no scan.

Day-to-day administration works by number. What the law requires to be kept is kept elsewhere, and contains nothing beyond what the law requires.

What the subscription supports

A fixed share of the house's result is assigned by statute to the training bursaries of the house next door.

Two stays of seven nights keep an apprentice for a whole year.

The member knows which craft he supports, and may choose it. He will not know the apprentice's name, and that will not change: a young man who knows himself kept by a powerful man ceases to be held by his house and becomes someone's protégé.

Once a year the member receives a handwritten line saying what was done — a panel laid, a door finished, a master kept. He may go and see it, and will find it in the same place in twenty years.

For any request — a stay, a presentation, a designation.
