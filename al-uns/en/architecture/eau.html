ARCHITECTURE

Water

Water organises the house: coolness, sound, purification, the path through the patio and the garden.

In the architecture of the riad, water is not an ornament added at the end. It structures the plan, cools the air, marks the time of the day and recalls a cosmology in which the garden is crossed by living streams.

PATIO

Basin and fountain

At the centre of the patio, water gathers and redistributes. The basin is a mirror and a climate machine: it cools, humidifies, softens the hard light of noon.

The sound of the fountain is part of the education of the house — not noise, but a continuous measure that holds silence without emptiness.

SALSABIL

Salsabil

The salsabil — a thin sheet of water running over a sloped plane — belongs to the classical vocabulary of Islamic architecture. It multiplies coolness and sound without waste.

At the Riad it is used where the path asks for it: thresholds, rest rooms, the approach to the garden.

GARDEN

Water in the garden

Channels, irrigation, the patient distribution of water to trees and beds. The kitchen garden and the main garden depend on this care as much as on the soil.

Learning to water is learning measure: neither scarcity nor excess.

HAMMAM

Water and purification

In the hammam, water is heat, steam, rinse, return to the body. The sequence of rooms is a pedagogy of temperature and time.
