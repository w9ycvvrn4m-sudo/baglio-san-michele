THE HOUSE

The building site

Here, the time of building

The first work is not an object to take away. It is the house itself — walls, stucco, wood, water — clothed by the ateliers of Dar al-Hiraf.

Until the house stands, the building site is the first atelier. One does not wait for a décor in order to begin forming. One forms by building.

THE FIRST WORK

The house as reception

Al-Athar says it: the first work left in the walls will be the house. Zellij, gebs, wood, iron, weaving — what Dar al-Hiraf teaches a man, he applies here first.

The building site is not a subcontractor. It is the place where the craft proves that it holds, before it has a market.

THE CHAIN

The ateliers clothe the house

The corporations of Fès and the ateliers of the Riad gradually supply fabrics, stucco, ceramics, wood, metal and light. The craft thus has a commissioner before it has a client.

The Munya follows the same law, on the other side of the common wall: the work of the ateliers clothes both houses, without confusing them.

THE RHYTHM

A nucleus, then growth

The house does not open with every corporation. The building site follows the same order: a nucleus of four or five ateliers, rooms that correspond to them, then the wing or the adjoining volume when the masters and the resources are there. The door toward the Munya remains untouchable.

WHAT REMAINS

When the building site falls silent

The wall ceases to wait. The work remains. The man who held it takes a malaka with him, not the house. One does not take the Riad away.
