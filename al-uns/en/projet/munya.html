PROJECT

Al-Munya

The adjoining residence. Selective hospitality next to the Riad.

Al-Munya is the house next door. It is not an extension of the Riad as a hotel, nor a separate project: it is the place where selective hospitality meets the life of the house without disturbing its formation.

Those who stay at Al-Munya are received according to the same principles of measure and respect. The door between the two houses remains intentional.

For the full presentation of Al-Munya: al-munya.com
