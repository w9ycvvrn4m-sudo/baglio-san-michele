GUEST HOUSE

Al-Munya

المُنْيَة

The Riad’s guest house — for guests of regard. Separate, discreet, few at a time.

Al-Munya is the neighbouring house reserved for those one neither refuses nor exhibits. It is not a hotel wing and is not visited as a tourist site. One is brought there.

Entrances, kitchens, staff and circulation are entirely separate from the Riad. The threshold between the two houses remains inviolable.

البيت جارٌ للبيت

The house is neighbour to the house.

The spaces

Sqifa

Entrance vestibule. A threshold space that prevents a direct view of the interior.

Guest apartments

Private rooms with their own service. A limited number.

Apartment of honour

A unit of higher rank and greater generosity of space.

Salons

Closed rooms for quiet conversation.

Patio and hall

The heart of the house. Porticoed patio and main hall opening onto it.

Garden

With water, shade and paths. Integral to the whole.

Oratory

Prayer space, correctly oriented, overlooking patio and garden.

Dining room

Served by an autonomous kitchen, fully separate from the Riad’s.

Parlour

First reception space, distinct from every room of the Riad.

Service rooms

Accessible only to Al-Munya staff. Invisible to guests.

قليلٌ من الفضاءات، كلٌّ في موضعه

Few spaces, each in its place.
