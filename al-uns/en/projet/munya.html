PROJECT

Al-Munya

A private house adjoining the Riad. Intended for a closed majlis — retreat, measured company, discreet hospitality.

Al-Munya is not a tourist annex of the Riad. It is a distinct house, with its own entrance, its own kitchen, its own circulations.

It allows what the Riad — a house of formation and of shared life — cannot offer: a space of regard, of silence and of measured reception, without confusing the two vocations.

A single shared wall. A door that can be opened only from the Riad side.

The spaces

Sqifa

The threshold. Transition from the street to the house.

Patio and hall

The heart of the house. Light, water, ordered space.

Garden

Shade, plants, quiet. An extension of the interior order.

Oratory

Facing the patio and garden. A place of prayer and recollection.

Guest apartments

Rooms prepared for those who are admitted.

Apartment of honour

For guests of particular regard.

Salons

Spaces of conversation and measured company.

Dining room

With its own kitchen. Autonomy of the house.

The rule of the two houses

Two houses, two entrances, two kitchens, two circulations. A single shared wall.

Al-Munya does not exist to take the place of the Riad. It exists to allow, in discretion, what the Riad neither can nor should be.

الرِّياضُ لِأَهْلِهِ، لَا أَهْلُهُ لِلرِّياضِ

« The riad is for its own, not its own for the riad »
