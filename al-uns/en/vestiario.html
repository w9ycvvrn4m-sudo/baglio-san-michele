MAGHREB

The Moroccan wardrobe

المغرب

جلابة

Djellaba

ǧallāba · jellaba

Long, loose-fitting robe with a pointed hood (قب, qob). Everyday and outdoor piece: cotton or linen in summer, wool in winter. The hood protects from sun and cold. The backbone of the Maghrebi men's wardrobe.

جبادور

Jabador

ǧabādūr · jabador

Ceremonial set: straight tunic closed by a row of hand-braided spherical buttons (عقد, ʿaqad / aakad), matched with serwal trousers (سروال, sirwāl). Mdama belt (مضمة, maḍamma). Weddings, feasts, great occasions.

قميص

Qamis

qamīṣ · kamis

Straight, light tunic, without a hood, reaching the ankles. Cotton or white satin. Often worn with the taqiyah. Indoor variant: the gandoura (غندورة, ġandūra).

سلهام

Selham

silhām · selham

Wide wool cloak, open, sleeveless, with a hood. Worn over the djellaba in cold weather or on horseback. A sign of dignity and protection.

طربوش · طاقية · عمامة · شاشية

Moroccan head coverings

ṭarbūš · ṭāqiyya / taguia · ʿimāma / rezza · chechia

Tarbouche (Fez) — cylindrical cap of red felt, often with a black silk tassel. Ottoman and Maghrebi urban heritage.

Taqiyah (Taguia) — small round skullcap, closely fitted to the head. The simplest and most common piece, worn for prayer as much as in everyday life. In Morocco, taguia.

Turban (ʿimāma / rezza) — length of cloth wound around the head or the taqiyah. A more solemn variant, still present in traditional circles.

Chechia — a form close to the taqiyah, softer, sometimes in wool or felt. Everyday and prayer use.

IN THE WORKSHOPS

Work attire

Short-sleeved tunic, hard-wearing fabric, leather belt, belgha — the garment that accompanies the gesture of the craft. Not for show: the hand free, the body at ease.

Cotton or light linen, straight cut. The same logic as the qamis, adapted to work in the workshop.

تحت اللباس · الليل

Under the garment · Night

At home and at night — No codified pyjamas. Gandoura or fine cotton qamis.

Layers under the tunic — Izar or underwear → serwal → qamis / gandoura → djellaba or jabador.

ACCESSORIES

Belgha · Serwal · Mdama

Belgha — leather babouche. Serwal — loose trousers. Mdama — belt.

Beyond the Maghreb

Gulf · Mashreq · Ottoman world · Persia

Thobe · Dishdasha · Kandura

ثوب · دشداشة · كندورة

Long everyday tunic. Ghutra held in place by the ʿiqāl.

Thobe · Keffiyeh

ثوب · كوفية

Long tunic with a keffiyeh (kūfiyya) draped over the shoulders — a variant of the Mashreq and the Levant.

Bisht

بشت

Ceremonial cloak, often trimmed with gold.

Salvar · Cepken · Fes

Salvar, cepken, kuşak, fez. No hooded djellaba here.

Qaba · Pirahan · Shalvar

قبا · پیرهن · شلوار

The qabā is the long coat, often open at the front.
