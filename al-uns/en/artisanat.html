DAR AL-HIRAF

Crafts of the Riad

Manual crafts transmitted as a path of character — not as a catalogue of leisure workshops.

At Dar al-Hiraf each craft is a school of the hand and of the self: calligraphy, zellige, plaster, weaving, wood, iron, leather, pottery, perfumes. One learns to hold a gesture for years, under a master, within the shared life of the house.

HARD MATTER

Earth, stone, metal, wood

Zellige & pottery

Cut tile, glaze, kiln. Geometry and patience. The hand learns the angle before the pattern becomes visible.

Plaster & mocárabe

Carved plaster, muqarnas. Soft matter worked until it holds light and shadow like stone.

Wood & inlay

Carpentry, carving, assembly. The grain of the wood teaches resistance and direction.

Wrought iron

Forge, heat, the blow of the hammer. Strength under control — the same demand as in the physical disciplines.

SOFT MATTER

Fibre, skin, scent, letter

Weaving & embroidery

Loom, thread, density. Time made visible in the cloth that will clothe the house and its residents.

Leatherwork

Tanning, cutting, stitching. Material that ages with the body that carries it.

Calligraphy

Maghrebi scripts, proportion, the ethical weight of the written word. The pen as discipline of attention.

Perfumes & essences

Distillation, composition, plant knowledge. Scent as memory of the garden and of hospitality.

PATH

From stage to residency

A short workshop can open a first contact with one craft. The long residency is the full commitment: years under a master, shared life, the clothing system of the house, the daily rhythm of prayer and work.
